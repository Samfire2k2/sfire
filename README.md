## ❄️ Minimalist Design & White Glow

- **Monochrome & Pure White**: Complete removal of red in favor of immaculate frosted glass, pure white accents and silver / zinc tones.
- **White Ambient Halo & Specular Reflections**: The light halo follows the mouse as a soft diffuse white glow (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`).
- **3D Micro-Tilt Cards**: Subtle, elegant hover parallax, with a white specular sheen sweeping across the glass surface.
- **Clean Header**: Avatar with glass ring, live pure white status indicator, `@samfire` handle, `Hello there` name and a discreet quote pill (*General Kenobi* with lightsaber Easter egg & silver confetti).
- **Minimal Audio Dock Player**: Pure white spectrum bars, clean progress bar and integrated Spotify toggle.
- **Spotlight Search (`Cmd+K` / `Ctrl+K`)**: Monochrome quick search modal.
- **Clean Lightbox**: Full-screen artwork inspection with zoom.
